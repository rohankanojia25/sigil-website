Features

Everything between "it compiles" and "it's safe."

01

Test generation

Full Foundry and Hardhat test suites from your contract, including the edge cases you didn't think of.

02

Exploit pattern matching

Your code checked against every published post-mortem and audit finding. Bugs that cost others millions get flagged before you deploy.

03

Local simulation

Fork the chain on your machine and see exactly what a call does: balances moved, storage changed. Read only. Nothing leaves your computer.

04

Gas optimization

Line-level suggestions with the exact gas saved, not vague advice.

05

NatSpec and docs

Complete documentation generated from your code, written like a human wrote it.

06

Cross-chain notes

Porting between Solidity, Rust and Move? Sigil explains what breaks and why: memory models, storage, call semantics.

How it works

Three steps. No setup marathon.

1

Point Sigil at a contract

A file in your repo or any deployed address on-chain.

2

It reads, checks and simulates

Plain-English explanation, exploit pattern matches, and a local read-only fork simulation of any call.

3

Ship with receipts

Generated tests, docs and gas notes go straight into your repo.
